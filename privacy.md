# Najd Music — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 20, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Najd Music ("the Bot") handles information when you interact with it on Discord.

## 1. Information the Bot May Process

Najd Music may process limited Discord information required to provide its functionality, including:

* Discord user IDs.
* Discord server (guild) IDs.
* Voice channel IDs.
* Text channel IDs when required for commands or responses.
* Commands and interactions sent to the Bot.
* Music playback and queue information.
* Configuration associated with a Discord server.

The exact information processed depends on the features being used.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information is processed only when necessary to:

* Operate Najd Music.
* Execute commands and interactions.
* Provide music playback functionality.
* Maintain server-specific settings.
* Diagnose errors and improve reliability.
* Prevent abuse or misuse of the Bot.

## 3. Data Storage

Najd Music may temporarily or persistently store limited configuration or operational information required for its functionality.

The Bot does not intentionally collect passwords, Discord account credentials, payment card information, or other unnecessary sensitive personal information.

## 4. Discord Authentication

Najd Music operates through Discord's platform and APIs.

Discord independently processes information according to its own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Najd Music does not control Discord's data practices.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Some functionality may rely on third-party services.

Those services may process requests or technical information according to their own privacy policies. Najd Music does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party services.

## 6. Data Sharing

Najd Music does not sell users' personal information.

Information is not intentionally shared with third parties except when necessary to operate requested functionality, comply with legal obligations, protect the service from abuse, or when required by the underlying platforms used by the Bot.

## 7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the operation, security, configuration, or maintenance of Najd Music.

Temporary operational data may be automatically removed when it is no longer required.

## 8. Data Requests

Users may request information regarding stored data associated with them or request deletion of applicable stored data by contacting the developer or official support channel for Najd Music.

Requests may require reasonable verification to prevent unauthorized access or deletion.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Najd Music is intended to be used through Discord and is subject to Discord's applicable age requirements.

The Bot does not knowingly collect personal information from users in violation of applicable age requirements.

## 10. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by Najd Music. However, no electronic service can guarantee absolute security.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to Najd Music, its functionality, or applicable requirements.

The latest published version will represent the current Privacy Policy.

## 12. Contact

Questions, privacy requests, or concerns regarding Najd Music may be directed to the developer or official support channel associated with Najd Music.
