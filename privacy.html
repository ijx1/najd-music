Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Najd Music ("the Bot") processes information when you interact with it through Discord.

2. Information We May Process

Najd Music may process limited information required to provide its functionality, including:

  • Discord user IDs.
  • Discord server (guild) IDs.
  • Voice channel IDs.
  • Text channel IDs when required for commands or responses.
  • Commands and interactions sent to the Bot.
  • Music playback and queue information.
  • Server-specific Bot configuration.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed to:

  • Operate Najd Music and provide its features.
  • Execute commands and interactions.
  • Provide music playback functionality.
  • Maintain server-specific settings.
  • Diagnose errors and improve reliability.
  • Prevent abuse or misuse of the Bot.

4. Data Storage

Najd Music may temporarily or persistently store limited configuration and operational information required for its functionality.

Najd Music does not intentionally collect Discord passwords, account credentials, payment card information, or other unnecessary sensitive personal information.

5. Discord

Najd Music operates through Discord's platform and APIs. Discord independently processes information according to its own terms and privacy policies.

Najd Music does not control Discord's independent data practices.

6. Third-Party Services

Certain functionality may depend on third-party services. Those services may process technical information or requests according to their own privacy policies.

7. Data Sharing

Najd Music does not sell users' personal information.

Information is not intentionally shared with third parties except where necessary to provide requested functionality, protect the Bot from abuse, comply with applicable legal obligations, or operate services used by Najd Music.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the operation, configuration, maintenance, reliability, or security of Najd Music.

Temporary operational information may be removed automatically when it is no longer required.

9. Data Requests

Users may request information regarding applicable stored data or request deletion of applicable stored data by contacting the developer or official support channel associated with Najd Music.

Reasonable verification may be required before fulfilling a request to prevent unauthorized access or deletion.

10. Children's Privacy

Najd Music is intended for use through Discord and is subject to Discord's applicable age requirements.

Najd Music does not knowingly collect personal information in violation of applicable age requirements.

11. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by Najd Music. However, no electronic service or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to Najd Music, its functionality, or applicable requirements.

The latest published version represents the current Privacy Policy.

13. Contact

Questions, privacy requests, or concerns regarding Najd Music may be directed to the developer or official support channel associated with Najd Music.
